## 贡献者公约（行为准则）

我们承诺让参与本项目的每个人都不受骚扰，无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济状况、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

我们承诺以促进开放、友好、多元、包容、健康社区的方式行事与互动。

**鼓励的行为**

- 对他人表现出同理心与善意；
- 尊重不同的意见、观点与经历；
- 给出并优雅地接受建设性反馈；
- 承担责任，向受影响的人道歉，并从中学习；
- 关注对整个社区最有利的事情，而不只是个人利益。

**不可接受的行为**

- 性化的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或挑逗；
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，人身或政治攻击；
- 公开或私下的骚扰；
- 未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息（真实姓名、住址、邮箱等）；
- 在专业场合中其他可被合理认为不当的行为。

**执行**

维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本公约的评论、提交、代码、Issue 与其他贡献，并在适当时说明理由。严重或反复违规将被临时或永久禁止参与。

需要举报时，请通过仓库所有者的 GitHub 主页所列联系方式私下联系维护者。所有投诉都会被及时、公正地审查与处理，并尊重举报人的隐私与安全。

本公约改编自 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1 版。

## Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

**Expected behaviour**

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people;
- be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- give and gracefully accept constructive feedback;
- accept responsibility, apologise to those affected, and learn from the experience;
- focus on what is best for the overall community.

**Unacceptable behaviour**

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement**

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code, and will communicate reasons when appropriate. Temporary or permanent bans apply for severe or repeated violations.

Report issues privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
